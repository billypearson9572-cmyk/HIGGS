# Andrew Bird Hearing — Digital Presence Audit

**Prepared ahead of my interview for the Marketing / IT Administrator role**
*An independent review of the current digital footprint, with prioritised, practical fixes I'd start on from week one.*

---

## Why I put this together

Before we met I wanted to understand the business the way a patient finds you online. So I reviewed the website, the location network, your Google and review presence, and your social channels. The headline: **the business itself is excellent and clearly trusted — the digital presence is selling it short.** Almost everything below is a quick, low-cost win rather than a rebuild. That's exactly the kind of work I'd love to own in this role.

---

## What's already strong (the foundation to build on)

- **A genuinely trusted brand** — independent since 2007, *"Looking after the South West's ears since 2007."*
- **An outstanding reputation** — 5★ from 92+ reviews. This is your single biggest marketing asset and it's currently underused.
- **A wide footprint** — Cheltenham HQ plus **17 satellite clinics** across Gloucestershire, Worcestershire, Herefordshire, Wiltshire, Bristol & Somerset, partnering with established opticians and medical practices, **plus home visits**.
- **A broad, high-intent service range** — hearing tests, hearing aids, ear wax removal (microsuction), repairs, hearing protection, and **industrial/B2B hearing testing**.
- **A clear, human tone of voice** — patients describe the team as *"charming, gentle and thorough."* That warmth should run through every asset; right now it lives mostly in the reviews.

## The gaps (and what I'd do about them)

### 1. Social media is the biggest, fastest opportunity 🔴 *High priority*

| Channel | Current state | The issue |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (@andrewbirdhearingltd) | **153 posts, but only ~62 followers** | A lot of effort going in, almost no reach coming out — this is a *strategy and format* problem, not an effort problem. |
| Facebook | ~512 likes, posting | Steady but flat; little video, limited local targeting. |
| LinkedIn | Company page exists | Barely used — yet it's the ideal channel for the **B2B/industrial testing** and **partner-clinic** side. |

**What I'd do:**
- Shift from static posts to **short-form video / Reels**. In hearing care, *ear-wax microsuction* content and *"what to expect at your appointment"* explainers perform exceptionally well and are highly shareable — with patient consent and tasteful editing, this is proven, low-cost reach.
- Build a **repeatable monthly content calendar** (education + patient stories + team + offers + local community) so posting is consistent and on-brand, not ad-hoc.
- Use the **92 five-star reviews as content** — turn them into branded quote graphics (templates included in this pack).
- Run **geo-targeted Facebook/Instagram ads** around each clinic town for low-cost local awareness (e.g. "Free hearing checks in *[town]*").

### 2. Local SEO — 18 clinics is a goldmine that isn't fully mined 🔴 *High priority*

You operate in 18 towns. Each one is a chance to rank for high-intent local searches like *"ear wax removal near me"*, *"microsuction [town]"*, *"hearing test [town]"* — searches made by people ready to book today.

**What I'd do:**
- Claim/optimise a **Google Business Profile for every clinic location**, each with correct hours, services, photos and review links. This is the #1 driver of local "near me" visibility and it's free.
- Make sure every location has its **own optimised landing page** (town in the title, address, services, local schema markup) rather than one shared list.
- Add **review (star) schema** so the 5★ rating can show in Google results — a major click-through booster.
- Build **local citations** (Yell, Bing Places, health directories) with consistent name/address/phone across all sites.

### 3. The website looks dated and under-converts 🟠 *Medium priority*

The current site (Squarespace) is functional but the design feels behind the quality of the service. First impressions matter for an older, trust-driven audience.

**What I'd do (no rebuild needed — I'd work within Squarespace):**
- Add a **persistent "Book an appointment" call-to-action** and click-to-call phone button on mobile.
- Refresh the visual brand for consistency (see the **brand refresh assets** in this pack).
- Surface **reviews, accreditations and "independent since 2007"** trust signals above the fold.
- Improve **accessibility** for an older / hard-of-hearing audience: larger text, strong colour contrast, captions on all video. (This is both right *and* good SEO.)
- Tighten page titles and meta descriptions for the core money pages (ear wax removal, hearing tests, hearing aids).

### 4. Reviews & reputation aren't working hard enough 🟠 *Medium priority*

92 five-star reviews is brilliant — but it should be **growing automatically and visible everywhere**.

**What I'd do:**
- Automate **review requests** by SMS/email after each appointment (gentle, one tap).
- Embed a live review widget on the homepage and key landing pages.
- Repurpose the best reviews into social graphics and ad copy.

### 5. Email & patient retention — a quiet revenue engine 🟠 *Medium priority*

Hearing care has a natural lifecycle: re-tests, wax-removal repeats, battery/accessory needs, and a **3–5 year hearing-aid upgrade cycle**. Most of that can be nurtured automatically.

**What I'd do:**
- Set up **email marketing** (e.g. Mailchimp) with automated reminders for annual checks, wax-removal follow-ups and upgrade-eligibility prompts.
- A simple monthly **newsletter** with hearing-health tips keeps the brand front-of-mind and drives repeat bookings.

### 6. The B2B / industrial story is almost invisible 🟡 *Opportunity*

**Industrial hearing testing** (workplace noise-at-work compliance) and the **partner-clinic network** are real B2B revenue lines that get almost no dedicated marketing.

**What I'd do:**
- A dedicated **B2B landing page + one-page PDF** aimed at HR/Health & Safety managers (Noise at Work Regulations compliance).
- Use **LinkedIn** to reach local employers and to strengthen relationships with the partner opticians/clinics.

## The IT side (this role is IT *and* marketing)

Hearing care means handling **special-category health data under UK GDPR** across 18 sites — so security and reliability aren't optional. In the role I'd want to make sure:

- **Data protection is watertight** — encrypted patient records, controlled access, secure booking system, clear retention policy.
- **Cyber Essentials certification** is in place (or pursued) — increasingly expected for health-adjacent businesses and great for trust.
- **Backups and continuity** are tested across all sites, not assumed.
- **Staff are trained** on phishing, passwords and data handling — the cheapest, highest-impact security control there is.
- **Consistent, well-maintained kit** at every satellite clinic so the team can work reliably wherever they are.
