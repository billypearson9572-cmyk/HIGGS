Interview Crib Sheet — Andrew Bird Hearing

Keep this to yourself — your one-page memory aid for the room.

Know the business cold

  • Independent hearing specialists, Cheltenham, since 2007. HQ at Hesters Way Community Resource Centre, Cassin Drive, GL51 7SU. Tel 01242 262551.
  • 18 clinics (Cheltenham HQ + 17 satellites) across Gloucestershire, Worcestershire, Herefordshire, Wiltshire, Bristol & Somerset — run in partnership with opticians and medical practices. Plus home visits.
  • Services: hearing tests, hearing aids, ear wax removal (microsuction), repairs, hearing protection, and industrial/B2B hearing testing.
  • Reputation: 5★ from 92+ reviews. Widex stockist. Andrew Bird also listed via BUPA/Nuffield.
  • Digital gap: dated website, undefined brand, ~512 Facebook likes, only ~62 Instagram followers despite 153 posts. Big, cheap upside.

Your strongest stories to tell

  1. "I researched you before today." Lead with the audit — it proves initiative and that you can actually do the job.
  2. Local SEO + Google Business Profiles for 18 towns = free, high-intent visibility. This is your sharpest, most specific marketing insight.
  3. Reels-first social (microsuction / patient stories) to fix the Instagram gap.
  4. Reviews as an asset — 92 five-star reviews should be growing automatically and shown everywhere.
  5. IT side: you take patient health data (UK GDPR special-category) across 18 sites seriously — security, backups, Cyber Essentials, staff training.
  6. B2B angle: industrial noise-at-work testing + the partner-clinic network are under-marketed revenue lines.

Likely questions → quick angles

  • "Why this role / you?" → You love that it's hands-on across both IT and marketing; you've already shown what you'd do (point at the pack).
  • "Squarespace experience?" → Comfortable building/maintaining; would refresh within Squarespace, no costly rebuild needed.
  • "How would you grow us?" → The "first five things" list in the audit.
  • "Data protection?" → Encryption, access control, tested backups, Cyber Essentials, staff phishing training.
  • "Weakness / gap?" → Pick something honest + how you'd close it fast. Show learning mindset.

Smart questions to ask them

  • What does success look like in this role at 6 and 12 months?
  • Which of the 18 clinics has the most growth potential right now?
  • Is the industrial/B2B testing line something you want to grow?
  • How are patient bookings and records currently managed across sites? (IT insight)
  • What's worked and not worked in your marketing so far?

Practical reminders

  • They asked about the 15-mile Cheltenham radius and own vehicle / 25% travel — be ready to confirm.
  • Bring this pack printed (logo, leaflet, audit summary) as a leave-behind. Hardly any candidate does this.
  • Dress smart, arrive early, phones on silent, smile — it's a warm, people-first business.
