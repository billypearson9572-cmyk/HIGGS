AI receptionist

Never miss another call. Your AI receptionist, live in three weeks.

An AI answering service for small businesses across the UK that picks up every call, message and form in seconds — books the job, takes the details and chases the follow-up while you get on with the work.

The phone is where you win or lose the job

When a customer calls and you're mid-job, driving, or with another customer, the phone goes to voicemail. They hang up and ring the next name on the list. For a small business, that's not a missed call — it's a lost job, a lost booking, a lost patient or a lost valuation.

An AI receptionist changes that. It answers every call on the first ring, any time of day, sounds like a member of your team, and does the things a great receptionist would do: takes the enquiry, answers the common questions, books it into your diary, and chases the follow-up. You get the message and the booking — without being glued to the phone.

What your AI receptionist does

  • Answers every call, WhatsApp message, web form and social enquiry — 24/7, in seconds, not minutes.
  • Answers the questions you get asked daily: prices, opening times, availability, "do you cover my area?"
  • Qualifies the enquiry so you only chase real leads.
  • Books appointments and jobs straight into your calendar.
  • Sends confirmations, reminders and follow-ups automatically.
  • Hands over to you the moment a human is needed — with the full context.

It's an AI answering service, not an answering machine. It holds a natural conversation, knows your business, and escalates when it should.

Built for how small businesses actually run

If you're a plumber, electrician, locksmith or builder — it books the job and captures the address while you're on the tools. If you run a clinic, salon or studio — it fills the diary and handles reschedules. If you run a local service business — it turns "we'll call you back" into "you're booked in."

It plugs into the phone number, WhatsApp and website you already have, and the tools you already use. No new hardware, no answering service scripts, no call-centre retainer.

What it sounds like

Your AI receptionist is set up in your voice, with your pricing, your services and your availability. It greets callers naturally, answers in plain English, and never puts anyone on hold. If it can't confidently handle something, it transfers the call or takes a message — it never guesses.

Callers get an instant answer instead of voicemail. That's the difference between a booking and a customer who's already rung someone else.

What you get back

  • Fewer missed calls turning into lost work.
  • Every enquiry answered, logged and followed up.
  • Hours back every week that used to go on phone tag and admin.
  • A diary that fills itself while you do the work.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI really answer my business phone?

Yes. It answers on the first ring, holds a natural conversation, and knows your services, prices and availability. It's configured to your business, so it speaks for you — not as a generic bot.

What happens when the AI can't handle a call?

It escalates. You set the rules: transfer to your mobile, take a message, or hand to a specific team member — always with the caller's details and the context of the call so nothing is lost.

Does it work with my existing phone number and tools?

Yes. It connects to the phone number, WhatsApp and website you already use, and books into your existing calendar or CRM. No new hardware.

How long does it take to set up?

Two to three weeks from kickoff, with two to three hours of your time in total. We scope it, build it, train you and switch it on.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

One fixed price per project, agreed before we start. Founding clients pay half the standard rate. Ongoing usage typically runs £30–£80 a month on your own accounts.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

It's a natural, human-sounding voice and it's transparent when asked. Most callers simply notice their call got answered instantly — which is exactly the point.
