For dental practices

A missed call at lunchtime is a patient who booked somewhere else.

An AI receptionist for dental practices that answers every call, books appointments straight into your diary, and chases reminders and recalls — so your reception team can focus on the patients in the chair.

Your phone is busiest exactly when your reception isn't

In a dental practice, the phone rings at the worst possible moments — while reception is checking in a patient, during lunch, or in the middle of a busy afternoon list. Every unanswered ring is a new patient booking with the practice down the road, or an existing patient who couldn't reschedule and simply didn't come back.

An AI receptionist for dental practices answers every call on the first ring, 24/7, and handles the admin your team doesn't have time for — booking, rescheduling, reminders and recalls — so your diary stays full and your front desk stays calm.

What it does for your practice

  • Answers every call, web form, WhatsApp and social enquiry instantly — 24/7.
  • Answers the questions you get daily: "are you taking NHS patients?", "how much is a check-up?", "do you have any appointments this week?"
  • Books appointments straight into your diary in real time.
  • Sends confirmations, reminders and follow-ups automatically.
  • Runs recall and hygiene reminders that bring patients back on schedule.
  • Reschedules cancellations and fills gaps — reducing no-shows and empty chairs.

Built for the way a practice runs

Your AI receptionist knows your services, your prices, your opening hours and your NHS/private status. It can triage an emergency from a routine enquiry, route a nervous patient to the right reassurance, and hand straight to your team whenever a human touch matters.

It plugs into your existing practice management software, so appointments land in your diary without re-keying, and everything is logged for the record.

Fewer no-shows, fuller diary

Most missed appointments aren't bad patients — they're forgotten ones. Automated reminders and easy rescheduling meaningfully reduce no-shows and DNAs, and every gap gets filled from your waiting list. For a practice running on tight margins, that's revenue you were already losing.

Compliant and confidential

The system is configured around GDC advertising guidance and UK data protection. It makes no clinical claims, triages rather than diagnoses, and treats patient data with proper care. Anything clinical goes straight to a human.
