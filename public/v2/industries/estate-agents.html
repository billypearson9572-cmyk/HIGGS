The highest-value call in your business is the one you're missing

When a vendor decides to sell, they don't email three agents and wait. They ring three agents — and they usually instruct the first one who picks up. Miss that call because you're on a viewing, at lunch, or out of hours, and the vendor simply moves down the list.

It's the same with the portal leads that land at 9pm. By the time someone gets back to them in the morning, they've booked a valuation with the agent who replied at 9.05pm.

An AI receptionist for estate agents answers every one of those calls and enquiries in seconds — around the clock, every day of the week — and books them into your diary while your competitors' phones are still ringing out.

What it does for your agency

  • Answers every call, Rightmove/Zoopla-style portal enquiry, WhatsApp and web form instantly — 24/7.
  • Qualifies vendor from buyer, landlord from tenant, so you never waste a viewing slot.
  • Books viewings and valuations straight into your diary and your CRM.
  • Chases the follow-up: confirmations, reminders, feedback calls, re-engagement.
  • Flags the hot vendor leads to you immediately with full context.

The result: you win the instruction, not just the enquiry.

Built around how an agency actually works

Your AI receptionist knows your branch areas, your fees, your stock and your process. It can answer "how much is my house worth?", "do you cover [area]?", "what are your fees?" and "can you come tomorrow?" — consistently, in your voice, without sounding scripted.

It works with the tools you already run on. Enquiries flow into your existing CRM, diary and inbox, so nothing changes for your negotiators except how many qualified appointments are waiting for them.

Out of hours is where you win

Most vendor enquiries happen outside office hours — evenings, weekends, lunchtimes. That's exactly when your competitors' phones go to voicemail. Your AI receptionist answers on the first ring, takes the property details, qualifies the lead and books the valuation for the next morning. You start the day with appointments booked, not voicemails to return.

Compliance without the worry

The system is configured to stay on the right side of the rules — no invented valuations, no misleading claims, no pressure. It answers honestly, and hands to a human the moment a conversation needs your judgement. Property data and GDPR are handled properly, and everything is logged.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI receptionist really book viewings and valuations?

Yes. It qualifies the caller first — vendor, buyer, landlord or tenant — then books the viewing or valuation into your diary and CRM in real time, and confirms it with the caller before the call ends.

What happens to out-of-hours portal enquiries?

They're answered instantly, 24/7. The AI captures the property, qualifies the lead and books the follow-up — so a 9pm enquiry becomes a booked valuation, not a voicemail you return in the morning.

Does it work with my CRM and the portals?

Yes. It integrates with your existing CRM, diary and email, so enquiries flow into the tools your negotiators already use. No new software to learn.

How does it tell a vendor from a buyer?

It asks a short set of qualification questions — are you looking to sell, buy, rent or let out — and routes the enquiry accordingly, flagging hot vendor leads to you immediately.
